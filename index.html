        08 — Before You Go

        Before you go.

        I don't say this often enough in plain words, so let me say it here where I can't interrupt myself.

        I noticed you before I had a reason — the way someone notices a change in light. Then admiration followed, specific and growing: how you listen without rehearsing, how you stay yourself in every room, how you make silence comfortable instead of empty.

        I keep discovering you. That is the truest sentence in this whole gift. Not a single decision, but thousands of small elections of attention — and every one of them lands in the same place. Time with you never feels wasted, even doing nothing. Especially doing nothing.

        I get days wrong. Distracted, short, late in the ways I'm trying to outgrow. You never keep score, and that mercy has changed me more than any lecture. I want to repay it practically: listening longer, asking better questions, remembering what matters to you without being asked, keeping the small rituals that are only ours.

        I don't promise perfection. I promise presence — to tell you when I'm afraid instead of going silent, to choose the honest sentence over the easy one, to keep choosing the same door, the same laugh, the same you, and to walk toward it faster each time.

        If years from now someone asks what I was most sure of, I hope the answer is uncomplicated: her. The way she laughed with her whole face. The way home learned her name. The way my attention, out of everyone and everything in this world, kept returning — and still does.

        Yours — completely, and without theatre.

        Still you. Every time.
