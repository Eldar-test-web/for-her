03 — Why you

Why it was
always you.

Calm

My mind is loud. You lower the volume without trying — coffee tastes better, traffic feels shorter, ordinary evenings feel arranged just for us.

Attention

You notice the small edits — when I am tired before I say it, when I am proud and pretending not to be. Being known like that is rare.

Gravity

I am curious about everything, but I orbit back to you. Not from habit. From recognition, as if some older part of me had already decided.

Ease

Home stopped being a place. It is the ten minutes on the sofa doing nothing, your head on my shoulder, both of us half-laughing at nothing.

Respect

I admire how you move — direct, kind, unwilling to be small. You make me want to be more deliberate, more honest, more worthy of the seat beside you.

Return

Given every road again, I would still take the one that leads to the same door, the same laugh, the same you — and I would walk it faster.

05 — Before you go

Before you go.

I do not say this often enough in plain words, so let me say it here where I cannot interrupt myself.

I like who I am when you are near. Not a larger version, not a performance — a quieter, truer one. You make carefulness feel natural. You make staying feel like an adventure, not a compromise.

There are days I get wrong — distracted, short, late in the ways I am trying to outgrow. You never keep score, and that mercy has changed me more than any lecture could. I want to repay it daily, in practical ways: listening longer, asking better questions, remembering what matters to you without being asked.

I do not promise perfection. I promise presence. I promise to tell you when I am afraid instead of going silent, to choose the honest sentence over the easy one, to keep the small rituals that are only ours.

If years from now someone asks what I was most sure of, I hope the answer is uncomplicated: her. The way she laughed with her whole face. The way home learned her name.

Yours — completely, and without theatre.
